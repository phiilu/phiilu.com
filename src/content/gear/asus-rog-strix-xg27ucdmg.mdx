What I really wanted was an Apple Studio Display with 120Hz, but it costs too much, and I could not have used it anyway. I need one monitor for the Mac mini, the work MacBook Pro and my gaming PC.

So I went with OLED. I use it around 90% for coding and 10% for gaming, and for text it is great. The downside is burn-in. I had to change a few things on macOS to be safe: the Dock and menu bar auto-hide and the wallpaper rotates. Let's see how long it lasts.
